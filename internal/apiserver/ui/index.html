Set up yt-manage

1. Connect an account

Run this in a terminal — it opens the Google consent flow in your browser:

yt-manage account add
If your Google Cloud consent screen is still in Testing mode, Google expires your tokens after 7 days and your accounts will silently disconnect. Publish the consent screen to keep accounts connected: Google Cloud Console → OAuth consent screen.

2. First sync

Mirror your channels, videos and playlists into the local database. Search then costs no YouTube API quota.

3. Pick a channel

Choose the channel you work with most. It becomes the scope for Videos, Episodes and Playlists (you can change it any time in the header).

4. Done

You are set up. Start with your videos — search is instant and costs no quota.

      Privacy
      
          
      Everything yt-manage mirrors — channels, videos, playlists, comments, schedules and
          history — is stored only on this machine, in the data directory shown
          above. Nothing is uploaded to any yt-manage server; there is no account with us and no
          analytics or telemetry.
      
          
      Your Google sign-in tokens are stored encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM)
          in that same data directory, and are only ever used to talk to Google on your behalf.
      
          
      The only network calls this app makes are:
      
          
        
            
      • Google OAuth & YouTube Data API (googleapis.com) — sign-in,
            syncing your channels, and the edits you approve.
        • 
            
      • YouTube thumbnails (i.ytimg.com) — your browser loads video
            thumbnails directly from YouTube's image CDN.
        • 
          
      
          
      Nothing else is sent anywhere else — no third-party services, no cloud sync, no
          tracking pixels.
